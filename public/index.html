THE METHOD, IN PLAIN LANGUAGE

From velocity to a move

01Learn what sells

Fit a pooled Tweedie model when LightGBM is available, correcting observed units by in-stock days. Festival, wedding, store tier and product features are included.

02Allocate with purpose

Use expected margin and urgency to prioritize shortages; a binary MILP selects among safe proposals when SciPy is installed.

03Prove each rule

Python checks the proposed moves against display minimums, protected S++ styles, 30-day donor cover, in-transit stock and store capacity.

04Keep a human in charge

Gemini can answer questions about computed results; it never calculates stock, edits a move, or approves one on a person's behalf.
